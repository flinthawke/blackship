Crete · Greece

Eastern Mediterranean · South of the Aegean

Crete is a large Mediterranean island where traces of Minoan civilization meet mountains and coastal scenery.

About this place

  • Crete is the largest island in Greece.
  • Knossos is a major archaeological site associated with the Bronze Age Minoan civilization.
  • The island faces the Libyan Sea to the south and the Aegean Sea to the north.
